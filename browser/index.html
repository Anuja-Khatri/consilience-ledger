consilience-ledger: ledger browser

Static page. Reads ../ledger/*.json by relative fetch. No build system. Serve the repo with any local file server, or publish on GitHub Pages.

The determinism split (D4): extraction is gated, not deterministic. From approved claim cards onward the ledger is byte-deterministic. Verdicts are computed against Lila's own instrument data, which is loaded at runtime and not committed. Nothing here claims Lila uses, wants, or endorses this work.

What you are looking at: round 1 of the public record. The claims below were extracted from the two accessible published papers of the closed-loop OER literature (an autonomous-lab paper and one open-access reference from it); claim ids begin with the source paper's arXiv id. A veto means the two claims' measurement conditions were not proven comparable, and every veto names its reason. Zero forks is the honest result on this corpus, not an absence of work: where conditions are unstated, this instrument refuses rather than fabricates.

Beyond this page: a database quality pass over 140,708 claim cards (n=140,708); planted-disagreement recovery 1.00 and 1.00 (n=33); false positives on null data 0.000 (n=1,228, q=0.05); a measured inter-lab floor of 90.7 mV (n=9); and a pre-run safety gate at about 20 ms with zero false blocks on 13,627 benign plans. Every number and its qualifier is registered in the repository's numbers lock: github.com/Anuja-Khatri/consilience-ledger, starting with FOR_AUTONOMOUS_LABS.md.
