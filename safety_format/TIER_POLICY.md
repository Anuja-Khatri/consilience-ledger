# TIER POLICY

> **STATUS: FINAL. Adopted by Anu, 11 Nov 2026.**

This safety format ships with public-standard EHS content only. It is a container and a compiler: it holds rules, grades them, and compiles them into machine checks. It does not supply hazardous knowledge and never will. In scope: instrument protection bounds, consumables budgets, and standard incompatible-chemical classes from widely published general safety references, each entry carrying its grade and source. Permanently out of scope: synthesis routes, precursor guidance, and any content that could enable harm; such content is absent by policy and enforced by a denylist scan. Curating real hazard content is the job of domain scientists at an operating lab. This repository ships the format and a benign demonstration seed, nothing more.
